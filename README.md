Ashen Front is a PC 2D action RPG. Explore a scrolling, procedurally tiled
battleground, fight floor-specific enemy tactics, earn XP and coins, upgrade
weapons, skills, and health, and customize your character. The selected region's
terrain persists as you progress between floors. The 150-floor campaign has
mythic minion bosses on floors 5, 10, 15, 20, and 25; their five clues open a
rescue mission. Each defeated minion gives three independent 25% shadow-binding
attempts. Bound beasts can be summoned with Q.

Rescuing the Demon King while Superhuman advances the campaign to floor 100.
Floors 101-150 feature winged and horned demons, gravity magic, and staff-wielding
demons; floor 150 ends with the Demon of Death and Desire and fire- and
ice-breathing yetis.

Move toward any edge to reveal more ground; enemies arrive visibly on the
starting battlefield and on newly revealed ground.

Controls: WASD or arrow keys to move, Space or left-click to attack, mouse wheel
or +/- to zoom the battlefield, 0 to reset zoom, Q to summon a bound shadow,
and F11 to toggle fullscreen. Health is shown as hearts. Visit the field
upgrade shop between encounters.
